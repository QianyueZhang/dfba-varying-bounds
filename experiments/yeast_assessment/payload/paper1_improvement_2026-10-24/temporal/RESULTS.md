# Retrospective temporal stress test and matched uptake diagnostic

The unchanged model fits 27 Exp1-labelled observations before published glucose exhaustion, using the original three-parameter bounds, residual scales, initial state and optimizer. The temporal origin is the published exhaustion-relative clock; model structure was previously explored with the full dataset. This is a retrospective temporal stress test, not blind external validation.

The fitted parameters are qG=10.73642812, qO=6.26901171 and ATP demand=12.67900679 mmol/gDW/h, with qE fixed at 10. The single declared start converged by the gradient-tolerance criterion after 14 optimizer function evaluations and 50 actual residual evaluations, with no failed training trials. The training objective is 0.00112547522. Only the pre-origin observations enter that objective; the source initial OD reconstruction uses observations at -7.5 and -6.5 h, both before the forecast origin.

**The primary full forecast failed and remains failed.** A separate, declared diagnostic retained the successful prefix through 6.04369879 h. The next infeasible RHS query occurred at 6.04926625 h, with glucose approximately zero and ethanol 0.01615601 g/L. The accepted endpoint and failed trial are different records; these times are not a certified earliest feasibility-loss event.

At that failed query, maximizing ATP flux after removing only the fitted ATP lower demand, with every other constraint unchanged, gives at most 12.51799708 mmol/gDW/h. This is below the fitted demand by 0.16100970; the diagnostic LP has maximum equality residual 4.0e-14. It supports insufficient maintenance capacity at the queried depleted-carbon state. The surviving prefix covers 239 of 256 eligible observations; 17 have no prediction. No full-window RMSE is reported, no future observations are used to refit, and no clipping or model change repairs the primary failure.

The fine-tolerance pre-origin residual Jacobian in log parameters has singular values 1.70325, 0.426270 and 0.0244927, with condition number 69.54. This local numerical diagnostic suggests unequal sensitivity of parameter combinations; it is not a confidence interval or a global identifiability theorem. The unsuccessful future forecast illustrates why a pre-phase fit alone does not establish post-phase adequacy of constant effective physiological parameters.

The originally planned matched uptake comparison is also complete. It uses the archived all-phase parameter estimates, without refitting, and compares the same unexpanded LP with glucose as a lower-bound capacity versus glucose forced to that lower bound. The capacity is active at all 301 matched states. Maximum trajectory differences for biomass, glucose and ethanol are 1.02e-12, 2.56e-12 and 2.55e-12 in their respective concentration units. Thus this experimental condition does not discriminate those two glucose-uptake interpretations. It supports the source-linked batch comparison and error accounting, while the reduced-network example supplies the separate demonstration that uptake assumptions can differ.
